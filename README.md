# Single-cell atlas of perinatal development of the pig brain
# We generated a single-cell atlas of perinatal development of the pig brain by profiling 877,273 single-nucleus transcriptomes from multiple brain regions across multiple time points spanning mid‑fetal to neonatal stages. This resource establishes the pig as a conserved and transcriptionally relevant model for human fetal brain development, revealing key principles of neurogenesis, neuronal migration, and gliogenesis. Notably, we constructed a spatiotemporal expression atlas of genes linked to neurodevelopmental and psychiatric disorders, providing a foundational resource to explore disease mechanisms and fetal growth restriction. Our findings highlight evolutionarily conserved cell populations and trajectories, bridging cross‑species insights into both normal brain development and disorder susceptibility.
